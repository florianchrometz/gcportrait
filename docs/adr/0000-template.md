# NNNN. Short decision title in the imperative or as a noun phrase

- **Status:** proposed <!-- proposed | accepted | superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md) | rejected -->
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD <!-- the day the status last changed -->
- **Deciders:** Florian Chrometz (maintainer) <!-- and anyone else who decided -->
- **Related:** ADR NNNN, skill or guide <!-- the ADRs, skills and guides this decision belongs with -->

## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, the constraints (technical, legal, product), and what is already
decided elsewhere. Facts, not arguments. Two to six sentences.

## Decision

What we do, stated so that a reviewer can check a change against it. Use "must" and "never" only for rules a
check or a reviewer actually enforces.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what other parts of the repository now depend on this, and which
check, test or file enforces it. Name the backlog item ([docs/backlog.md](../backlog.md)) that delivers
anything not built yet.

## Alternatives considered

Each option that was weighed, with the one-line reason it lost. Leave this section out when no alternative
was considered.
