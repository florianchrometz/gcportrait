A zone you opened or closed by hand keeps that choice while the current lens or filters leave it empty: "Expand all" and "Collapse all" no longer undo it, so after switching to a lens that empties the zone and back again, it is as you left it. On a timeline day both buttons count the ghosts of what is gone since the day before: "Collapse all" is available when going back to Auto would fold a ghost project into a chip, even when nothing else on screen would change.
