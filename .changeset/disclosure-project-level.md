"Expand all" and "Collapse all" work as their names say in a view of one project as everywhere else: "Collapse all" closes the zones you opened and keeps the ones you closed (it no longer reopens them), neither button touches the zones of another drill-in or focus, each is offered only when it would change the diagram on screen, and a press that comes while a new view is still being drawn waits for that view instead of acting on the previous one. A double-click on a chip drills in without leaving the chip flipped behind.
