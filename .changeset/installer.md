Self-hosting without a clone: `curl -fsSL https://gcportrait.app/install.sh | bash -s -- --project YOUR_PROJECT` is the whole quick start. The installer, served by the website from `infra/install.sh` as it is, downloads the deploy scripts of the newest release (or of `--version vX.Y.Z`), checks their SHA-256 against the release's `checksums.txt` before it unpacks or runs anything, keeps them in a folder of their own and runs their `infra/deploy.sh` with the release's image; every other flag passes through to deploy.sh, and its exit status is deploy.sh's. Every release now attaches those scripts as `gcportrait-deploy-vX.Y.Z.tar.gz` and a `checksums.txt` over all its assets, and its notes carry the installer line. deploy.sh needs only `--project` and ends with the next steps, first and in order: open the address, create the OAuth client (the console link and the exact origins), redeploy with the exact command plus `--oauth-client-id`; what the check of the instance saw is shown with the new `--verbose`, and the commands it prints name the scripts by their path. The user guides say what to do: their verification records, dated notes and timed runs moved to `docs/history/`, and `node scripts/user-docs.mjs` keeps such records out of them. The self-hosting guide asks only for the project id, the OAuth client id and the email address to grant; its other commands use the defaults. The Cloud Shell tutorial takes the same steps; the "Open in Cloud Shell" button stays the alternative for those without a terminal.
