On phones and tablets the keyboard focus ring of the timeline's slider (and of the Filters' depth slider) goes round the visible thumb rather than its larger touch area, so at the first or the last day it no longer covers the start or end date beside the track; the touch area stays 44 × 44 pixels, and on a desktop the dates keep a little more room from the ring as well.
