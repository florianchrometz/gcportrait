On a phone or a tablet with the details open, the node you picked and the fitted drawing no longer end up under the Expand all and Collapse all row: the camera keeps them in the part of the canvas the row leaves free, and on a phone turned sideways (or the smallest phones upright), where the row would leave too little of the canvas above the details sheet, the row steps aside until the sheet closes.
