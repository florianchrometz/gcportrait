The diagram answers your fingers on a phone or a tablet as it answers a mouse on a desktop. One finger pans, keeping what you touched under it, and never selects; two fingers pinch to zoom about the point between them and pan as they move; a tap selects a node, clears the selection on an empty spot or opens a collapsed project, as a click does; a double tap zooms in where you tap, and on a collapsed project leaves it opened, as one tap does; pressing and holding a node for half a second previews its connections and shows its name by your finger, as resting a mouse on it does, without selecting it, until your next touch, and on the selected node's "Open in Cloud Console" button it shows the button's label and leaves your browser's own menu for the link. A mouse and a pen work exactly as before, also on a device with a touch screen, and the page around the diagram zooms with two fingers as any page does.
