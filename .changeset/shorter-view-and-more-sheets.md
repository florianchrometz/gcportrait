On a phone or a tablet, the View and More sheets over the diagram are at most two thirds of the diagram's height, so the lower third of the diagram stays in view below an open sheet and you see the drawing change as you pick a lens, a focus, a filter, a detail level or a theme. What does not fit scrolls inside the sheet. On a phone the "N of M shown" bar steps aside while a sheet is open, so that strip shows the drawing itself, and comes back when the sheet closes. On the other pages and on a desktop nothing changes.
