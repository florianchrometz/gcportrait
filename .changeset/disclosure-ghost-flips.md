On a timeline day, "Collapse all" no longer opens the ghost of a zone you closed by hand, and "Expand all" no longer closes one you opened: a ghost's own expand or collapse is kept or let go as a zone's is. Both buttons also judge the ghosts by the day they come from, so the ghost of a removed perimeter, or of something inside a project that has moved since, no longer makes a button available that would change nothing (or the other way round).
