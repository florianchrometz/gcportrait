On a phone the Snapshots page shows each stored snapshot, and each entry of a scope's timeline, as a card instead of a table row that had to be scrolled sideways to be read: the card lists the row's facts under the table's own headings (scope, read time, source, assets, size and when it was stored; or read time, what it is, assets and size), with the same Load, Export and Delete buttons under them, each at least 44 × 44 pixels. The button that deletes a scope's cached days wraps its words there, so nothing on the page scrolls sideways from 320 pixels up. Tablets and desktops keep the tables.
